## 🎁 Multi-Modal Components

### 1️⃣ Sentiment Component
- **Method**: VADER (Valence Aware Dictionary and sEntiment Reasoner)
- **Data**: 538 Reliance-related news articles
- **Output**: Sentiment score (-1 to +1) + classification (positive/neutral/negative)
- **Impact**: Ranked #1 most important feature for predictions

### 2️⃣ Technical Component
- **Indicators**: 13 different technical metrics
- **Types**: Momentum (RSI), Trend (EMA, MACD), Volatility (ATR), Volume
- **Purpose**: Capture price patterns and oscillations
- **Benefit**: Identifies support/resistance and overbought/oversold levels

### 3️⃣ Deep Learning Component  
- **Model**: LSTM (Long Short-Term Memory)
- **Architecture**: 2 layers (128 + 64 units) with dropout
- **Input**: 30-day sequences of all 15 features
- **R² Score**: 0.8428 (best single model)
- **Advantage**: Learns complex non-linear temporal patterns

### 4️⃣ Ensemble Component
- **Method**: Weighted averaging (GBR + Ridge + SVR)
- **Weights**: 50%, 30%, 20% respectively
- **Purpose**: Reduce overfitting and improve robustness
- **Result**: More stable predictions across market conditions
